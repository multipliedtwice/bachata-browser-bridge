Bachata may keep explicit local state and local logs required for operation and troubleshooting. It must not transmit those records for product measurement, diagnostics, profiling, crash collection, or usage analysis.

Network access is limited to functionality explicitly initiated and configured by the user, including supported model/provider connections, the authenticated local Browser Bridge, and an explicitly configured semantic interpreter endpoint. These functional connections must never be repurposed for measurement or reporting.

Any contribution that adds or proposes telemetry or unnecessary hosted platform infrastructure must be rejected.
